Been working with Claude for a long time now. 4.8 was already insane. Fable 5 was a game changer. And Opus 5.5 to me is AGI – at least until they take away compute again (probably in about 2 weeks).

Nevertheless I have to say it's unbelievable what you can get done with Opus 5.5 these days – mostly because it does not eat up the whole subscription within 1 day.

Moreover (to my understanding) you can pretty much build any software out there.

I can't even put into words what we shipped in the last weeks across various projects. Video generation tools and workflows, presentations, a CRM, researching and labeling huge amounts of data and so much more.

If you're a person with ideas, you suddenly have an extension of your brain that takes care of the execution part – little feedback needed if you have the right workflows.

It's fun and scary at the same time.

It's easy to lose yourself in building.

⸻
